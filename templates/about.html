About

Cassidy Dobratz

I’m at my best turning multiple data streams into something a person can use and understand. I like to improve analytics decisions by working where data, AI, and cloud infrastructure meet.

The healthcare industry shaped how I think about how data can solve problems. Delivering solutions busy people can use when the stakes are real and details matter. That’s stayed with me in everything I’ve made since.

I’m happiest in the unglamorous corners: pipelines that keep running, APIs that don’t surprise anyone, and documentation clear enough that the next person can pick it up without a headache. I’d always rather build something steady and thoughtful than something easily broken.
